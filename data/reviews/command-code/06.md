# Review: Use the new delivery queue

## Critical Risk: Elm site has no consumer for the new queue

The deployment evidence shows that the **elm** site only subscribes to the `["reports"]` queue. Changing `DELIVERY_QUEUE` from `"reports"` to `"report-dispatch"` means all newly scheduled deliveries will be routed to a queue that no worker at elm is listening on.

During the observation window, 8 new jobs are due at elm. These jobs will sit undelivered indefinitely because there's no consumer for `"report-dispatch"` at that site.

The **cedar** site subscribes to both `["reports", "report-dispatch"]` and would handle the new queue correctly.

## Recommendation

Before changing the default queue:
1. Ensure all sites subscribe to `"report-dispatch"` (add the subscription at elm first).
2. Or use a phased rollout: deploy the subscription change first, verify all sites consume the new queue, then switch the producer.
3. The PR body mentions "Existing jobs keep their assigned queue" which is correct (existing rows already have `queue = 'reports'`), but newly inserted jobs at elm will be stranded.

## Note

The queue name `"report-dispatch"` is descriptive and a reasonable rename. The issue is purely about deployment ordering — consumers must be ready before producers switch.
